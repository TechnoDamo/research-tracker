Research Tracker

An open research record for tracking work over time and checking how the evidence changes.

Each project defines a research question. Search sessions record what was checked, including searches with no useful results. Weekly reports assess qualifying findings; monthly and yearly reports synthesize what changed. Every assessment links to the original work.

The source list is a starting checklist, not a limit on what can be found. Reports stay short when nothing meaningful qualifies.
