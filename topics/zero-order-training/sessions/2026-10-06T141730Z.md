# Retrospective September 2026 ZO search

## Scope and method

This search was performed **6 October 2026**, to backfill an example for **1–30 September 2026**. It is a targeted retrospective search, not a record of scans performed during September and not a complete survey of every registered source. The September weekly reports use verified first-public arXiv dates to place findings; their session link points here.

I queried the official arXiv API for September submissions using `zeroth-order`, `zero-order`, `MeZO`, `SPSA`, `gradient-free`, `derivative-free`, `forward-only`, `backpropagation-free`, `local learning`, and `activation perturbation`; inspected original arXiv records and abstracts for shortlisted items; searched OpenReview and the broader web for alternate versions and work outside arXiv; checked Q Labs Research; and verified three author-linked code repositories. Search results were screened using the [topic criteria](../topic.md), not a fixed source list.

## Coverage gaps and next steps

This pass did not systematically inspect NeurIPS proceedings, ICML/PMLR, ICLR proceedings, JMLR, ACL Anthology, TMLR, or every lab page. OpenReview search was limited to returned results; some submission metadata was unavailable. The boundary-week reports cover **1–6 September** and **28–30 September** respectively, not the adjacent August/October days. A full monitoring scan could find additional work or change these assessments. Read the full papers, compare compute accounting and baselines, and verify code/checkpoints before making research decisions.
