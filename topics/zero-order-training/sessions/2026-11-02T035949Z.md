## Updates to known work

- `zero-order-training-2026-10-24-noise`: official merged [PR #42](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise/pull/42) on Oct 22 reports 1.58 s to loss 4.2 in the old 10M-token track; the [lab update](https://qlabs.sh/noise) summarizes roughly 4,000x improvement from 1.76 h. This is a fixed small benchmark, with timed update interval excluding validation and permitted untimed kernel warmup, not a general pretraining throughput result.
- The benchmark moved to 30M tokens after Oct 22. [PR #48](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise/pull/48) merged Oct 26 (open-architecture loss 3.2115); [PR #52](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise/pull/52) merged Oct 28 (speedrun to 3.5 in 22.9 s); [PR #54](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise/pull/54) merged Oct 30 (fixed-architecture loss 3.2998 in 43 min, research five-seed mean 3.3014). These are distinct 30M rules, not comparable with the 10M speedrun target.
- `zero-order-training-2026-11-02-zo-muon`: arXiv v3 dated Oct 28 now reports ViT-L rather than the prior ViT-B result; further version-to-version audit needed before attributing all gains to new experiments.
- GRZO and ZeroQAT have earlier preprints, so the October event is **conference publication**, not method invention.
