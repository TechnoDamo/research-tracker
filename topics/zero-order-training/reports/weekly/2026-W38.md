### <span id="zero-order-training-2026-10-06-compo"></span>zero-order-training-2026-10-06-compo — A Zeroth-Order Paradigm for LLM Preference Alignment

- **Authors:** Peter Chen, Xi Chen, Wotao Yin, Tianyi Lin.
- **Publication/release date:** 2026-09-16.
- **Source / venue:** arXiv (`arxiv`); preprint.
- **Publication type:** preprint.
- **Canonical URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19144
- **Importance:** 3/5.
- **Relevance:** high.
- **Evidential maturity:** 3/5.
- **Contribution:** Comparison-based Preference Optimization (ComPO) extracts directional information from preference pairs through comparison oracles instead of directly optimizing a differentiable preference loss on them. The authors analyze offline and online variants and report length-controlled win-rate improvements across Mistral, Llama, Gemma, and Qwen model families.
- **Why it matters:** It extends the possible uses of ZO methods from supervised fine-tuning to preference alignment, where the feedback signal may be comparative.
- **Evidence and source quality:** Original 39-page preprint with stated convergence and performance guarantees plus first-party experiments. The guarantees rely on smoothness, gradient sparsity, oracle compatibility, coverage, and reward-accuracy conditions described by the authors.
- **Major limitations:** The link from comparison-oracle theory to real preference quality depends on those conditions; independent replication and full compute comparison were not established in this search.
- **Code / project URL:** None verified.
- **Status / update:** New preprint, catalogued retrospectively on 2026-10-06.
