# Weekly Research Review — 2026-W44 (Oct 26 – Nov 1)

## Executive Summary

- **Most consequential change:** The 30M-token Noise benchmark recorded a fixed-architecture validation loss of 3.300 (Oct 30) and a 22.9-second speedrun to loss 3.5 (Oct 28); its open-architecture track reached 3.211 (Oct 26).
- **Conference evidence:** GRZO and ZeroQAT appeared in the Oct 24–29 EMNLP 2026 proceedings; these methods had earlier preprints and are **not** October inventions.
- **Revision:** ZO-Muon v3 dated Oct 28 extends its reported vision evaluation to ViT-L.
- **Adjacent result:** FedZOO exact-unlearning theory and experiments appeared Oct 26.
- **Conclusion:** Structured perturbations, batchwise variance reduction and credit-assignment/optimizer co-design are active directions, but a compute-matched large-scale ZO pretraining breakthrough remains unverified.

## High Priority — directly relevant

### <span id="zero-order-training-2026-10-24-noise"></span>[Noise: 30M-token records and open-architecture track](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise)

- **Authors:** Serdar Gülbahar, Bishwas Mandal, Akshay Vegesna, Samip Dahal (benchmark); record contributors including CharlesDDDD, RobertTLange, and others.
- **Publication/release date:** 2026-10-12; substantive event: 2026-10-30.
- **Source / venue:** Official Q Labs GitHub benchmark and merged PRs #48, #52, #54.
- **Publication type:** project_release.
- **Canonical URL:** https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise
- **Importance:** 5/5.
- **Relevance:** high.
- **Evidential maturity:** 2/5.
- **Contribution:** After the Oct 22 shift to 30M tokens, fixed-architecture track 1 reached validation loss 3.2998 in 43 minutes (PR #54 merged Oct 30), versus reported backprop baselines 3.419 with Adam and 3.340 with ANVIL II. Track 2 reached loss 3.5 in 22.9 seconds (PR #52, Oct 28); the new open-architecture track reached 3.2115 (PR #48, Oct 26).
- **Why it matters:** This is a concrete, code-backed test of estimator, optimizer, architecture and hardware co-design; the 30M regime is a harder benchmark than the first 10M speedrun.
- **Evidence and source quality:** [Official 30M rules/records](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise), [PR #48](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise/pull/48), [PR #52](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise/pull/52), [PR #54](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise/pull/54). PR #54 reports a five-seed research mean of 3.3014; its official run is seed 0. The open-architecture score is not directly comparable to the fixed-architecture track.
- **Major limitations:** Small fixed model and dataset, permissive architecture/initialization rules, benchmark-specific tuning, one official seed and limited independent scrutiny. Track-2 timing excludes validation and allows untimed warmup; 10M and 30M thresholds must never be compared as the same task.
- **Code / project URL:** https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise
- **Status / update:** Material update to [W42 Noise launch](../weekly/2026-W42.md#zero-order-training-2026-10-24-noise) and [W43 speedrun](../weekly/2026-W43.md#zero-order-training-2026-10-24-noise); record event Oct 26–30.

### <span id="zero-order-training-2026-11-02-grzo"></span>[GRZO: Group-Relative Zeroth-Order Optimization for Large Language Model Fine-Tuning](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1711/)

- **Authors:** Liyan Tan, Yequan Zhao, Yifan Yang, Ruijie Zhang, Xinling Yu, Zheng Zhang.
- **Publication/release date:** 2026-06-01; substantive event: 2026-10-29.
- **Source / venue:** Findings of EMNLP 2026, Oct 24–29 proceedings; original arXiv preprint June 1.
- **Publication type:** conference_paper.
- **Canonical URL:** https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1711/
- **Importance:** 4/5.
- **Relevance:** high.
- **Evidential maturity:** 3/5.
- **Contribution:** Uses Flipout-style pseudo-independent per-example perturbations and group-relative normalization to obtain multiple directional estimates from two model forwards. Reports +3.0 average accuracy points on Llama3-8B over MeZO and improvements across sparse/low-rank/quantized ZO variants.
- **Why it matters:** Batch-dimension variance reduction is an orthogonal candidate to head/embedding-only FO, structured directions and preconditioning in the current research agenda.
- **Evidence and source quality:** [Official EMNLP paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1711.pdf) includes theory, Llama3-8B/OPT-13B/RoBERTa-large tests and system measurements; on an RTE example the paper reports 23% more per-step time than MeZO while near the inference memory floor. The venue event ran Oct 24–29; **Oct 29 is the verified conference end, not a verified paper-upload day**.
- **Major limitations:** Fine-tuning, not from-scratch pretraining. Pseudo-independent directions need scrutiny for covariance and implementation cost; two forward passes do not guarantee equal wall-clock time. No independent replication verified.
- **Code / project URL:** None verified in this session.
- **Status / update:** Older [June 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02857) published in October EMNLP proceedings; first assessed Nov 2, recorded here as Oct 24–29 venue event, not a new October invention.

### <span id="zero-order-training-2026-11-02-zo-muon"></span>[Powering Up Zeroth-Order Training via Subspace Gradient Orthogonalization (ZO-Muon)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17155v3)

- **Authors:** Yicheng Lang, Changsheng Wang, Yihua Zhang, Mingyi Hong, Zheng Zhang, Wotao Yin, Sijia Liu.
- **Publication/release date:** 2026-02-19; substantive event: 2026-10-28.
- **Source / venue:** arXiv v3 revised Oct 28, 2026; original Feb 19 preprint.
- **Publication type:** preprint.
- **Canonical URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17155v3
- **Importance:** 4/5.
- **Relevance:** high.
- **Evidential maturity:** 3/5.
- **Contribution:** Combines low-rank projected ZO estimation with Muon-style spectral orthogonalization. The Oct 28 v3 abstract reports 24.7% of MeZO's queries to reach the same SST-2 result and a 25.1% accuracy improvement on ViT-L CIFAR-100.
- **Why it matters:** Directly informs tests of direction cosine, structured subspaces, matrix conditioning and ZO-vs-FO update geometry.
- **Evidence and source quality:** [Original arXiv v3 record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17155v3) confirms revision Oct 28 and revised ViT-L claim; method/theory and LLM/ViT experiments are first-party. The earlier version referred to ViT-B, so the evaluation scope changed.
- **Major limitations:** Not new in October; a v3 revision is not independent validation. Full v2-v3 diff, query-to-wall-time conversion and optimizer fairness require audit. No from-scratch LM pretraining result.
- **Code / project URL:** None verified in this session.
- **Status / update:** Substantive Oct 28 revision to a Feb 2026 work; first assessed Nov 2.

### <span id="zero-order-training-2026-11-02-zeroqat"></span>[End-to-End On-Device Quantization-Aware Training for LLMs at Inference Cost (ZeroQAT)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.28/)

- **Authors:** Qitao Tan, Xiaoying Song, Ningxi Cheng, Guoming Li, Jun Liu, Lingzi Hong, Caiwen Ding, Jundong Li, Xiaoming Zhai, Shaoyi Huang, Jin Lu, Ninghao Liu, Wei Niu, Geng Yuan.
- **Publication/release date:** 2025-08-21; substantive event: 2026-10-29.
- **Source / venue:** Findings of EMNLP 2026, Oct 24–29 proceedings; earlier arXiv preprint 2025.
- **Publication type:** conference_paper.
- **Canonical URL:** https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.28/
- **Importance:** 3/5.
- **Relevance:** high.
- **Evidential maturity:** 3/5.
- **Contribution:** Jointly optimizes quantized weights and quantization parameters using forward-only finite-difference estimates, with a lightweight variant retaining only selected full-precision Q/V weights. Reports 13B low-bit adaptation within 8GB and OPT-6.7B fine-tuning on a OnePlus 12 smartphone.
- **Why it matters:** Relevant to memory-limited ZO systems and the interaction between quantization and perturbation noise, although it does not establish competitive from-scratch language-model pretraining.
- **Evidence and source quality:** [Official EMNLP paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.28.pdf), tables and device experiments; official conference dates Oct 24–29. The earlier [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00031) predates October. Oct 29 marks the conference end, not an independently verified online publication day.
- **Major limitations:** Quantization-aware adaptation of existing models, not evidence of full-precision ZO pretraining parity. Very low-bit quality varies substantially by task/setting; no independent replication found.
- **Code / project URL:** None verified in this session.
- **Status / update:** October 2026 conference publication of older work; first assessed Nov 2.

### <span id="zero-order-training-2026-11-02-fedzoul"></span>[Efficient Exact Unlearning for Federated Zeroth-Order Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35211)

- **Authors:** Weijun Yin, Yanqing Yao, Falko Dressler, Di Wang, Youming Tao.
- **Publication/release date:** 2026-10-26.
- **Source / venue:** arXiv preprint, Oct 26, 2026.
- **Publication type:** preprint.
- **Canonical URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35211
- **Importance:** 3/5.
- **Relevance:** medium.
- **Evidential maturity:** 2/5.
- **Contribution:** Introduces TV-stable federated ZO learners and coupled exact-unlearning procedures, proving distributional equivalence to retraining under the designed algorithms. Reports up to 56.1% lower cumulative unlearning time and roughly four orders less communication than selected first-order baselines.
- **Why it matters:** A concrete systems/theory application of seed-coordinate ZO updates and stability, but not an improvement to central LLM pretraining efficiency.
- **Evidence and source quality:** [Original arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35211) with theorems and experiments; the exact-unlearning claim is about matching the retrained learner's distribution, not restoring an identical deterministic model.
- **Major limitations:** Federated/unlearning setting; gains are not matched ZO-versus-FO pretraining results. Reuse/coupling assumptions and communication baseline choices need independent testing.
- **Code / project URL:** None verified in this session.
- **Status / update:** New Oct 26 preprint; first assessed in Nov 2 catch-up.
