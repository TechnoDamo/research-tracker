# Weekly Research Review — 2026-W43 (Oct 19 – Oct 25)

## Executive Summary

- **Most consequential change:** Noise's 10M-token speedrun fell from 1.76 hours to 1.58 seconds by Oct 22, a >4,000× reduction in the benchmark's timed update interval.
- **Second finding:** A specialized compact wavelet network was trained with CMA-ES without backpropagation on Oct 21.
- **Evidence:** Noise has dated merged code/PR records; neither result demonstrates transformer-scale generalization or a compute-matched FO pretraining win.

## High Priority — directly relevant

### <span id="zero-order-training-2026-10-24-noise"></span>[Noise: 10M-token backprop-free speedrun improved over 4,000×](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise)

- **Authors:** Serdar Gülbahar, Bishwas Mandal, Akshay Vegesna, Samip Dahal (benchmark); community PR contributors.
- **Publication/release date:** 2026-10-12; substantive event: 2026-10-22.
- **Source / venue:** Q Labs Noise project, official merged GitHub PRs #40 and #42.
- **Publication type:** project_release.
- **Canonical URL:** https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise
- **Importance:** 5/5.
- **Relevance:** high.
- **Evidential maturity:** 2/5.
- **Contribution:** In the fixed 37.7M-parameter GPT/10M-FineWeb benchmark, merged Oct 22 PR #42 reached validation loss 4.2 in 1.58 seconds of timed updates on 8×H100, versus the original Dust baseline of 1.76 hours. PR #40 reached loss 3.676 on the quality track; the lab summarizes a greater-than-4,000× speedrun improvement.
- **Why it matters:** It changes the practical estimator-and-systems optimization agenda for backprop-free pretraining and supplies inspectable implementations of Hadamard-cycle probes, identity-path credit and specialized kernels.
- **Evidence and source quality:** Official [Oct 22 merged PR #42](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise/pull/42), [PR #40](https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise/pull/40), [Q Labs account](https://qlabs.sh/noise), and archived 10M rules. Results are first-party benchmark submissions, not independently replicated scientific comparisons.
- **Major limitations:** Only 37.7M parameters and a fixed 10M-token task; timer excludes validation and permits untimed kernel warmup. Faster time to loss 4.2 is not a claim of full-epoch pretraining in 1.58 seconds. One official seed and a heavily tuned public benchmark; no foundation-scale transfer demonstrated.
- **Code / project URL:** https://github.com/qlabs-eng/noise
- **Status / update:** Update to [original W42 assessment](../weekly/2026-W42.md#zero-order-training-2026-10-24-noise); Oct 22 record. The 10M track closed when rules changed after Oct 22.


## Medium Priority — useful adjacent work

### <span id="zero-order-training-2026-11-02-wavelet-forward"></span>[Forward-Only Learning for Shared-Kernel Wavelet Neural Networks without Backpropagation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29697)

- **Authors:** Yongfei Guo, Xuan Sun, Ang Li, Zhao Zhang, Yuanhao Gong.
- **Publication/release date:** 2026-10-21.
- **Source / venue:** arXiv cs.LG preprint, Oct 21, 2026.
- **Publication type:** preprint.
- **Canonical URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29697
- **Importance:** 3/5.
- **Relevance:** medium.
- **Evidential maturity:** 2/5.
- **Contribution:** Applies CMA-ES with structure-aware decomposition and channel-specific credit assignment to a compact shared-kernel wavelet reconstruction network. Authors report BSDS500 MSE 4.013 versus 4.178 for Adam/BP and six training-set coverages versus 1,500 BP epochs.
- **Why it matters:** Provides a small-network example where search-space structure, rather than sheer population size, enables competitive forward-only optimization.
- **Evidence and source quality:** Original [arXiv manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29697) with multi-seed and structural ablations; the model is a specialized compact image reconstruction network.
- **Major limitations:** Epoch counts are not a compute-normalized comparison; CMA-ES population evaluation cost and architectural specificity make extrapolation to transformers/LLM pretraining unjustified. No independent replication found.
- **Code / project URL:** None verified.
- **Status / update:** New Oct 21 preprint; first assessed in this Nov 2 catch-up.
